Aquarium

Sofia Efremenko

This series of works shows the futile and one-sided bond created between an observant pedestrian and an absent-minded driver, as their lives briefly intersect at a red light crossing. The driver is shielded by the glass-paned windows of their car and hence does not notice the inquisitive look of the pedestrian. It’s the thin glass separating the two individuals that shifts the dynamic of this relationship, a pedestrian is out in the world, the driver too but the latter gets a false sense of privacy by virtue of technically being inside (of their car).

Sofia Efremenko is a contemporary figurative painter whose work often centers on portraiture. Through her paintings, she captures fleeting, everyday moments in quiet defiance of the social media age's obsession with perfection and spectacle. Her practice reclaims the beauty and significance of life’s small, often overlooked events.
