Triptych – Strict Parent, Innocent Child, Fragile Adult

Daria Izvolskaya

This triptych draws on the Jungian model of inner figures — the Child, the Adult, and the Parent — as a system of interconnected states that shape the internal architecture of the self.

Each figure is presented as an autonomous object; however, the meaning of the work unfolds precisely through their coexistence. They are neither hierarchical nor isolated. On the contrary, a tense relational field emerges between them, in which each figure inevitably influences the others, as their relationships unfold within the subject.

Innocent Child embodies vulnerability, primordial openness, and dependence.

Fragile Adult captures a state of unstable equilibrium — a figure compelled to care, endure, and adapt simultaneously.

Strict Parent appears as an internal authority of control, norms, and limitations, establishing boundaries while also capable of exerting pressure.

Daria Izvolskaya is a self-taught artist with a background in architecture. She explores form, structure, and material presence and works primarily with ceramics, while also engaging with sculpture and mixed media. Her practice investigates archetypes, embodiment, and internal transformation through references to Slavic folklore and pagan symbolism.
