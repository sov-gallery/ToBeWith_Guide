OURBOROS. The Power of Two

Ornella Orlandini

Ouroboros represents the energy of the ancient symbol of the serpent. This energy does not come from a single body, but from the relationship between two. Like mating snakes, whose bodies twist together to form a living loop, each exists through the other. Life emerges in shared space and constant contact, where the relationship itself becomes a source of continuous renewal.

A series cyanotypes toned with the use of natural materials is arranged into a composition where each print on it’s own as well as the composition as a whole are creating loops, repeats and patterns, emphasizing interconnectedness and distinctness on several different planes.

The work is part of the artist’s ongoing project on the Sacred Feminine, but this piece also speaks of the vital energy that is released through relationships.

Ornella Orlandini is a lens-based artist living in Berlin. She is studying and practicing shamanism, and her artistic research centers on themes of self-awareness and feminine consciousness in relation to the environment and nature. Adopting a performative approach, she integrates theoretical research with experiential exploration, creating photographic works rooted in intimate performances and self-portraiture.
