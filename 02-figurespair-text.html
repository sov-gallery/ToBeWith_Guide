Figures Pair

Lonni Wong

Wong’s work explores the question of how we are all connected, driven by a fascination with the coexistence of differing values and qualities. She investigates the shared ground between the familiar and the seemingly foreign, asking where the feeling of disorientation begins and whether displacement might serve a purpose.

The works presented in this exhibition evoke the sense of navigating unknown territory—spaces that are both right and wrong, revealing hidden truths through subtle disguises. Beauty becomes a space of tension and delight, where half-seen meanings emerge and dissolve. In these shifting landscapes, each step feels both unfamiliar and strangely close to home.

Through the exploration of both duality and individualization, Wong’s works elicit both serenity and upheaval, depending often on nothing more then context and arrangement.

Lonni Wong is a Berlin-based ceramic artist of Chinese-German heritage. Her sculptural work explores selfhood and interconnectedness, weaving together biomorphic textures, organic forms, and vibrant surfaces. Embracing the unpredictability of the clay medium, she sees her process as a reflection of a world in constant flux—where transformation occurs even as deeper truths remain disguised.
