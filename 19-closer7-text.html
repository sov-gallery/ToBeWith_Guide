Come Closer 7

Johanna Brummack

The painting series "Come Closer (physical time and quality touch)" emerged from the artists interest in the way closeness evolves, how we define it, and what we expect from it in a society where relationships are increasingly understood as a trainable skill and a field of personal achievement. Genuine intimacy also means friction, tension, and admitting uncertainty. Concepts such as “the five love languages” promise guidance and connection, but also carry the risk of reducing relationships to an empty, standardized formula.

The scenes depict closeness as an ambivalent experience – a fragile process between care and control, desire and unease that can shift at any moment.

Johanna Brummack (they/them) works as an artist and teacher. They combine video, sound, ceramics, text, and fabrics to create physically tangible spaces of encounter and exchange. Their visual-sensory research and various theoretical approaches such as queer theory, listening cultures, and trauma research inform their interest in the interplay of biological, psychological, and social phenomena in processes of learning and unlearning.
