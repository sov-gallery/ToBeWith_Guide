FUUUUUUCK

Marcin Gajewski

Part of a series of playful wooden works, this text-based sculpture aims to express universally familiar feelings through a shared lens of viewing habits and cultural codes. ‘fuuuuuuck’ exposes the irony of our longing for genuine emotion within the confines of “authenticity culture” and meme-ification of everyday experience — rendering even the most complex and meaningful emotions cookie-cutter ready and optimized for engagement and recognizability metrics.

At once flattening and universalizing emotion, these shared codes are also desperate attempts to create community and collective vulnerability within the increasingly isolating social experience of the digital world.

With the transposition of this imagery into a haptic material form, combined with the skilled manual production process, Gajewski both delivers an ironic commentary and a a compassionate homage to our shared struggle for connection, depth, and belonging, as we enter an age of frictionless experience and digital alienation.

Marcin Gajewski is a Berlin based artist and filmmaker working across media to explore what it feels like to be human in today’s digital world. His recent text-based explorations are seeking to establish an emotionally honest language on nuanced and complex feelings.
