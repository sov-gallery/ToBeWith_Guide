Mother's Instinct

Margarita Montgomery

This painting presents love as a biological and inescapable instinct that overrides self-preservation. A mother fish, half-alive, is witnessing her children devour her own flesh in a sacrificial act ensuring the survival of the next generation. Care appears here not as comfort, but as a harsh reality common to all living beings.

Margarita Montgomery is a painter currently based between Berlin and Hamburg. Drawing on dreamlike imagery and subconscious landscapes, her work explores emotional states such as anxiety, loneliness, and existential uncertainty. Combining human, animal, and object-based figures within surreal, atmospheric settings, she investigates the complexities of the human condition with a strong sensitivity to color, shape, and emotional tone.
