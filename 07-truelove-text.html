In Search of True Love

Polina Trishkina

Through building a tactile wall sculpture entirely from discarded single-use plastics, Polina Trishkina explores the tension between comfort and crisis. By reshaping what is usually ignored, she turns it into new material language, a reflective surface for new modes of awareness. It forces us to feel what we’d rather overlook. Transforming waste into delicate forms initially tied to protection, warmth, and healing, the artist weaves together the symbolism of a blanket with the uncomfortable legacy of consumption. The blanket becomes a metaphor of how we cover both personal solace and the planet at once—seeking ease while hiding the consequences of our actions and obscure habits. The wall mimics geological formations—crystalline, organic, but unmistakably synthetic, and invites visitors to touch it, smell it, examine closely. In doing so, the material reveals itself not just as garbage but as a new kind of substance—seductive, layered, deeply implicated.

Polina Trishkina works with unconventional materials such as salt, single-use plastics, water, light, and found objects. Her practice evokes a kind of future archaeology. Combining transparency, crystallization, and media artifacts, she reflects on time, memory, and the ephemeral nature of existence.
